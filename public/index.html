Refund & Cancellation Policy

At M J DIGITAL, customer trust is our priority. Below are the terms governing order cancellations and refunds:

  • Product Cancellation: You can cancel any physical product order directly from your Account Dashboard before dispatch without any penalty or cancellation fee.
  • Return & Replacement: We offer a 7-day replacement guarantee on mobile devices and electronics in cases of physical transit defect, seal tampering, or technical faults upon arrival.
  • Travel Package Cancellation: Tour cancellations requested 15 days prior to scheduled departure are eligible for an 80% refund. Cancellations between 7–14 days receive a 50% refund. Cancellations made within 7 days of departure are non-refundable due to airline and hotel commitments.
  • Refund Processing Timeline: Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original payment source (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Netbanking) within 5 to 7 business banking days.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data protection are fundamental to M J DIGITAL:

  • Information Collection: We collect necessary transactional information including customer name, phone number, shipping address, and email solely to process orders, fulfill delivery, and provide customer support.
  • Payment Security: We do not store credit/debit card numbers or bank credentials on our servers. All monetary transactions are processed through RBI-authorized, encrypted payment gateways.
  • Third-Party Sharing: Customer details are shared only with logistics carriers and airline/hotel partners to execute service fulfillment. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.
  • Data Protection: We use industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL) to safeguard your data against unauthorized access.

Terms & Conditions

  • Order Acceptance & Pricing: All listed prices for smartphones, electronics, apparel, and travel services are inclusive of applicable taxes unless specified otherwise. We reserve the right to cancel or modify bookings in cases of typographical pricing errors.
  • User Accounts & 2FA: Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login and OTP credentials. All activities under your account are your sole responsibility.
  • Jurisdiction: Any dispute or claim arising out of orders or services shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

At M J DIGITAL, customer trust is our priority. Below are the terms governing order cancellations and refunds:

  • Product Cancellation: You can cancel any physical product order directly from your Account Dashboard before dispatch without any penalty or cancellation fee.
  • Return & Replacement: We offer a 7-day replacement guarantee on mobile devices and electronics in cases of physical transit defect, seal tampering, or technical faults upon arrival.
  • Travel Package Cancellation: Tour cancellations requested 15 days prior to scheduled departure are eligible for an 80% refund. Cancellations between 7–14 days receive a 50% refund. Cancellations made within 7 days of departure are non-refundable due to airline and hotel commitments.
  • Refund Processing Timeline: Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original payment source (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Netbanking) within 5 to 7 business banking days.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data protection are fundamental to M J DIGITAL:

  • Information Collection: We collect necessary transactional information including customer name, phone number, shipping address, and email solely to process orders, fulfill delivery, and provide customer support.
  • Payment Security: We do not store credit/debit card numbers or bank credentials on our servers. All monetary transactions are processed through RBI-authorized, encrypted payment gateways.
  • Third-Party Sharing: Customer details are shared only with logistics carriers and airline/hotel partners to execute service fulfillment. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.
  • Data Protection: We use industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL) to safeguard your data against unauthorized access.

Additional Terms & Details

Supplementary compliance guidelines for specific products and services:

  • Hardware Warranty: All brand-new smartphones and electronic gadgets are covered under official manufacturer brand warranty across India.
  • Travel Documentation: For international tours, customers must possess a valid passport (minimum 6 months validity) and appropriate travel visas.
  • GST Invoices: Valid GST Tax Invoices are generated for every completed purchase and can be downloaded from your account dashboard.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

M J DIGITAL ensures fast and secure delivery across India:

  • Dispatch Window: All in-stock smartphones, electronics, and fashion apparel are securely packed and dispatched from our Danilimda, Ahmedabad headquarters within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Transit Duration: Standard courier transit across major metro cities and towns takes approximately 3 to 6 working days.
  • Insured Shipping: All shipments are fully insured against transit damage.

MJ Pay / Wallet Terms & Conditions

Terms governing the use of M J Pay and M J Wallet services:

  • Wallet Usage: M J Wallet allows customers to store funds securely for 1-click instant recharges, BBPS utility bill payments, and store checkouts.
  • Top-Up & Settlement: Wallet top-ups via UPI QR, Credit/Debit cards, or Netbanking are credited instantly.
  • BBPS Compliance: Utility recharges are processed through authorized Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) aggregators.
